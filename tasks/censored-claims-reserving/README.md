# Censored claims reserving

Estimate outstanding catastrophe indemnity and pooled attachment exposure from a censored longitudinal cohort. Claims belong to one of two unobserved populations. Independent catastrophe effects sit inside a persistent Gaussian calendar process, so separate events and successive cohorts remain dependent after conditioning on observations.

The agent gets historical and current observations and reporting-pool membership. Historical observations determine the common marginal maximum-likelihood fit. The forecast conditions on the entire history and current cohort at those fixed parameters. Later current observations can inform an earlier cohort, and settled or out-of-pool claims remain evidence. Requested monetary totals contain future payments of the listed open claims only.

`solution/reserving.py` implements the censored observation likelihood, event-effect integration, analytic scores, capped moments and joint predictive sampling. `solution/calendar_filter.py` integrates the calendar chain with normalized forward/backward quadrature and posterior transitions. The fit is refined over event and calendar quadrature orders. Pool moments retain within-event covariance, common-cohort covariance and cross-cohort covariance. Four separately scrambled, fixed-seed Sobol predictive replications estimate attachment probabilities.

`solution/check_reference.py` independently implements the observation likelihood and calendar filter on uniform grids in probability space. It shares the predictive sampling routine; independence concerns the likelihood implementation, numerical posterior integration and filtering. It compares those forecasts with the protected reference. Run it with `python3 solution/check_reference.py` from the task directory. The script is a reference-development check, not part of grading.

The analytic likelihood gradient agrees with central finite differences to relative error below 2e-7. Forward/backward likelihoods and state/pair marginals agree with explicit enumeration of a small state chain to machine precision. Capped moments agree with direct severity-density integration to relative error below 2e-15.

The agent image copies only `environment/data`. The generator, solution and protected references are outside it. Background: [Claims reserving, state-space models and the Kalman filter](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-institute-of-actuaries/article/abs/claims-reserving-statespace-models-and-the-kalman-filter/2FD054402F1CF1C644F0C94C8938EE87) develops state-space claims-reserving methods; [Calendar year effect modeling for claims reserving in HGLM](https://arts.units.it/handle/11368/2961796) examines dependence and prediction uncertainty from origin and calendar effects.
